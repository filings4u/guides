Privacy

The screenings4u Guide Recorder is used by authorized screenings4u staff to capture workflow screenshots from screenings4u-owned portal domains and save them to the screenings4u Guide Builder.

The extension records only interactions made while recording is explicitly active. Captured data can include page URL/title, clicked control label/selector, click coordinates, and a screenshot of the visible tab. Fields marked as sensitive and common password/SSN/payment fields are masked before capture.

Captured guide data is sent only to screenings4u's Supabase backend for Guide Builder storage. The extension does not sell data, use data for advertising, or transmit data to unrelated third parties.
